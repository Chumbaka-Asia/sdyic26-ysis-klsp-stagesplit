Keyboard shortcuts

On a Mac, use ⌘ Cmd instead of Ctrl. Inside a text box, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V and Ctrl+Z work on the text as usual.

Ctrl /Show this list

Earlier versions

Your story is saved automatically, and a copy is kept every couple of minutes. Versions you saved with a name are marked ★ and are never cleared away. Pick one to bring it back. Your current version is kept too, so you can always change your mind.
